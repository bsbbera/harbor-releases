Refunds

Last updated 18 November 2026

If Harbor Pro isn't right for you, write to us within 14 days of buying and we'll refund the full price. No questions asked.

How to ask

Email support email, added before launch with your order number or the email you bought with. You can also use the link in your Lemon Squeezy receipt.

What happens next

  • The refund goes back to the card or account you paid with, usually within 5 to 10 business days.
  • Your licence key is turned off. Harbor keeps working as the free version, and none of your files change.

After 14 days

Write to us anyway if something is wrong. We look at each case.
