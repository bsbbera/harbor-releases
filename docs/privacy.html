Privacy

Last updated 18 November 2026

Short version: Harbor does not collect your files, your photos or how you use the app. There are no analytics and no ads.

What stays on your Mac

Everything Harbor learns about your files stays on your Mac: folder sizes, photo search data, culling scores and cleanup explanations. Photo search, culling and explanations run on your Mac using Apple's frameworks or models you download.

When Harbor goes online

  • Update checks. About once a day Harbor downloads a small list of versions from GitHub. You can turn this off in Settings › General. GitHub sees your IP address, like any download.
  • Licence checks (Pro only). Harbor sends your licence key and your Mac's name to Lemon Squeezy to activate it, and the key about once a week to check it.
  • Optional downloads. If you choose to, Harbor downloads the photo search model from GitHub and the assistant model from Hugging Face.

Purchases

When you buy Harbor Pro, Lemon Squeezy collects your name, email and payment details to process the order and send your key. We receive your name, email and order details from them, and use them only to support your purchase. See Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy.

Email

If you write to us, we use your email only to reply. We don't send newsletters unless you ask for them.

Your rights

You can ask us to show or delete what we hold about your purchase at support email, added before launch.
